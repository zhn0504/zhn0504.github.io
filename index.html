Biography

I am currently a B.Sc. student in Mathematics at the University of Auckland, as part of a joint program with Northeastern University. My current research focuses on multimodal large language models (MLLMs), particularly diffusion-based MLLMs, with an emphasis on multimodal reasoning, visual grounding, and hallucination mitigation.

Previously, I worked on computer vision problems including image restoration, RGB-event visual tracking, and spatio-temporal modeling. I am currently seeking PhD/MPhil opportunities for Fall 2027 or Spring 2028.
